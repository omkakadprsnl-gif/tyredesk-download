ANDROID SETUP

Downloaded the APK? Here's what to do.

One-time installation
  1. 1
    Tap the download notification

    After the file finishes downloading, swipe down from the top of your phone and tap TyreDesk.apk.

  2. 2
    Allow installation if asked

    If Android blocks the installation, choose Settings, allow installs from this browser, then go back.

  3. 3
    Tap Install or Update, then Open

    Sign in to open your shop. If TyreDesk is already on your phone, install this update over it. Future releases are available under Settings → Update App.

Can't find the download?

Open your browser's Downloads list or your phone's Files → Downloads folder, then tap TyreDesk.apk. Installation always needs your confirmation.
