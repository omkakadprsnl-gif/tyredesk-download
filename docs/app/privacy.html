TyreDesk Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11-21.2 · Effective 21 November 2026

Provider: TyreDesk. A private support/privacy email has not yet been supplied for this release. Do not send confidential information through public support.

Scope and responsibilities

This policy describes the current local TyreDesk app and download website. Your shop determines why and how customer and staff information is used in its business. Customers should contact the shop shown on their invoice for access, correction, deletion or other applicable requests. The software maintainer does not routinely receive or remotely access your local shop database.

What is stored and why

TyreDesk stores shop and owner contact details, GST and invoice details, a salted derived PIN verification value, optional logo and payment QR, customers' names and phone numbers, vehicle registrations, invoices, payment records and references, inventory, suppliers, expenses, audit entries, reminder titles, notes and due times. These support billing, stock management, dues, reminders and recovery. Setup also stores the accepted terms and privacy versions and device-recorded acceptance time; this is not customer consent or independent proof of identity.

Where information goes

TyreDesk saves records first on this device. If the owner enables shop cloud sync, TyreDesk sends business records, settings, the saved payment QR and pending reminders to the shop’s own Supabase project. Signed-in devices using the shared shop account can read and change those records. Supabase processes authentication, network requests and database storage. The device stores a renewable login session. PIN verification values, local audit entries and backup files are excluded from sync. The shop controls account access, backups and deletion in its project. Signing out stops sync on that device. The public website and update downloads are hosted by GitHub Pages; ordinary hosting requests may expose IP address and request information to GitHub under its own policies. Payment QR images are processed on the device and synchronised when shop cloud sync is enabled.

Optional sharing and backups

WhatsApp sharing opens a prepared message and requires you to send it; WhatsApp then handles shared data under its policies. Printing, exported files and manually selected Google Drive backups share information with the destination you choose. Local and Drive backups contain business and personal records and the payment QR; they are not encrypted by TyreDesk. Protect the files and destination account. A customer scanning your QR uses their payment provider, and the QR may reveal your merchant/payment identity. TyreDesk does not request UPI PINs, bank passwords or card credentials.

Retention, deletion and security

Records remain on your device until you delete them, replace them through restore or clear app/site data. Completing a reminder permanently removes it from the current database; older external backups can still contain it and restoring one may bring it back. You control backup retention and deletion. The app PIN limits casual access but is not database encryption. Use a device lock, current software and restricted access. Uninstalling or clearing storage may erase everything; export needed statutory records first.

Choices, requests and incidents

Collect only what your shop needs, explain its purposes to customers and provide the permissions and rights required by applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection framework as provisions take effect. Correct local records where supported, minimise sensitive notes and handle lawful requests subject to statutory retention. To stop local processing, stop use and clear app/site data after preserving legally required records. Delete external copies separately. There is no remote deletion service for local databases. If a device or backup is compromised, secure it, assess the affected information and fulfil applicable notification duties.

Children and changes

TyreDesk is intended for adult business operators and is not directed at children. Avoid collecting children's information; if necessary, meet applicable parental permission and other safeguards. New cloud or processing features will require updated disclosure and appropriate choices before activation.

Contact and legal review

Customers: contact your shop using its invoice contact details. Operators: use the installation support contact or the public TyreDesk issue tracker for non-confidential product questions only. Never attach personal data or backups there. The provider should publish its verified legal identity and a private privacy/grievance contact, and obtain professional Indian legal review before commercial distribution. This policy is transparent product documentation, not a guarantee of legal compliance.
